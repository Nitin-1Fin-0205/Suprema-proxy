## ⚡ QUICK DEPLOYMENT

1. **Copy** entire folder to client machine (e.g., `C:\SupremaProxy\`)
2. **Right-click** → **Run as Administrator** → **INSTALL.bat**
3. **Done!** Service auto-starts and runs forever
